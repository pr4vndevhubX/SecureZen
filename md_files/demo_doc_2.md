SIEM vs SecureZen

Passive vs. Proactive: SIEMs are "log libraries" that wait for you to search them; SecureZen is an Intelligence Layer that actively triages threats for you.
Raw Data vs. Actionable Insights: A SIEM gives you thousands of cryptic logs (noise); we use Generative AI to provide human-readable summaries and clear next steps.
High vs. Low Cognitive Load: Traditional SIEMs require analysts to write complex queries; SecureZen uses RAG-powered assistance to automate the investigation.
Collection vs. Decision: A SIEM's job is to collect the "dots." SecureZen's job is to use AI to connect the dots so you can make faster decisions.
